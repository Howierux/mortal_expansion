# Dreamcatcher

Craft one with four Sticks in the corners and five Ectothread in the remaining slots:

```
Stick  Thread Stick
Thread Thread Thread
Stick  Thread Stick
```

Hang it on a sturdy vertical block face. The player placing it becomes its owner; breaking and placing it again assigns the new placer. Losing wall support drops the item. Owner UUID persists in block entity data. Unowned decorations (such as command-placed blocks) provide no protection.

While at least one owned Dreamcatcher is loaded anywhere on the same server, its placer may sleep despite nearby monsters. This overrides only NOT_SAFE through NeoForge CanPlayerSleepEvent; daytime, obstructed/distant beds and invalid dimensions retain their normal rules. Other players are unaffected. No chunk tickets are created; unloading or breaking the last owned decoration stops both benefits immediately.

Each selected implemented Haunt event has one 50% coin flip to be caught, whether positive or negative. Multiple decorations do not stack. A caught event shows a hint and is handled without rerolling, invoking its effect or queuing a ghost. Natural event cooldowns and research spending remain as for a handled selection. This includes Secrets of the Dead: the Dreamcatcher can catch a research-funded revelation, spending that completed research charge without learning the secret. Deferred ghost delivery and targeted test commands also honor it; Dreamcatchers do not cancel an already active effect.

The Dreamcatcher recipe and book-map branch share the existing Ectothread secret bit. Ectothread revelation teaches both recipes. The new node connects from Ectothread in Secrets of the Dead and remains hidden until that revelation and the ectoplasm book upgrade. Existing learned Ectothread knowledge also qualifies; login re-awards the new recipe through the existing knowledge synchronization. No new secret bits or book/save migrations are required.

Block, item, recipe, loot, axe tag and models are generated by Java providers.
