# Dreamcatcher

Craft one with four Sticks in the corners and five Ectothread in the remaining slots:

```
Stick  Thread Stick
Thread Thread Thread
Stick  Thread Stick
```

Hang it on a sturdy vertical block face. The player placing it becomes its owner; breaking and placing it again assigns the new placer. Losing wall support drops the item. Owner UUID persists in block entity data. Unowned decorations (such as command-placed blocks) provide no protection.

While at least one owned Dreamcatcher is loaded anywhere on the same server, its placer may sleep despite nearby monsters. This overrides only NOT_SAFE through NeoForge CanPlayerSleepEvent; daytime, obstructed/distant beds and invalid dimensions retain their normal rules. Other players are unaffected. No chunk tickets are created; unloading or breaking the last owned decoration stops both benefits immediately.

Each selected implemented Haunt event has one 50% coin flip to be caught, whether positive or negative. Multiple decorations do not stack. A caught event shows a hint and is handled without rerolling, invoking its effect or queuing a ghost. Natural event cooldowns and research spending remain as for a handled selection. This includes Secrets of the Dead: the Dreamcatcher can catch a research-funded revelation, spending that completed research charge without learning the secret. Deferred ghost delivery and targeted test commands also honor it; Dreamcatchers do not cancel an already active effect.

The Dreamcatcher recipe and book-map branch share the existing Ectothread secret bit. Ectothread revelation teaches both recipes. The new node connects from Ectothread in Secrets of the Dead and remains hidden until that revelation and the ectoplasm book upgrade. Existing learned Ectothread knowledge also qualifies; login re-awards the new recipe through the existing knowledge synchronization. No new secret bits or book/save migrations are required.

## Purified Dreamcatcher

Upgrade one regular Dreamcatcher with one Purified Ectothread in any crafting grid, or craft directly with four Sticks in the corners and five Purified Ectothread in the remaining slots. Both produce one Purified Dreamcatcher. Both recipes share the existing Ectothread secret with the regular version; a new book-map entry branches from Dreamcatcher and describes guaranteed protection. Purified Ectothread itself retains its existing knowledge requirement.

A loaded owned Purified Dreamcatcher guarantees interception of all implemented good and bad Haunt effects, including secret revelations. It retains regular ownership, wall placement, sleeping, unload/removal and event-spending behavior. Strongest owned loaded variant wins (0%, 50% or 100%); multiple decorations do not stack. Unloading/breaking the purified variant falls back to 50% if a regular one remains loaded. No random draw is needed for guaranteed protection. Both blocks share the existing Dreamcatcher block entity type and Owner field; block identity determines strength, without changing saved data.

## Live moon-phase display

Both placed Dreamcatcher tiers show a small symbolic lunar charm centered on the wooden hoop. Flat crescents, half-disks and gibbous shapes follow all eight native phases; full moon is a filled disk and new moon is a dark disk inside a pale outline. The regular tier uses pale sage ink and the purified tier uses icy white-blue ink. There are no lunar surface details, sky texture, bloom or square backdrop. The item sprites retain their existing artwork.

The shared client-only block-entity renderer draws small untextured color quads using Minecraft's rendering API. Circle spans are cached, and the native level moon-phase API chooses the terminator. The charm follows time changes and sleep, stays legible in darkness without emitting block light, and rotates with all four wall facings. Protection, ownership, sleeping rules, recipes and saved data remain unchanged. No ticker, packet, image asset, custom shader or mixin is added. On version updates, verify phase zero is full, phase four is new and the model-facing anchor is preserved. Build and mod-loading checks cover registration; final appearance on all walls remains a client playtest check.
