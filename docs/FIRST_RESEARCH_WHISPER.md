# First Secrets of the Dead whisper

The Mortality Research Station is now the first secret in revelation order. A player carrying even the starting Book of Ectcetera can receive its whisper at Natural personal Haunt or higher. Bone/Raw/Purified upgrades and sentience are not required for this first revelation. It teaches the station recipe, writes knowledge into that book and shows a whisper hint with a quiet soul-escape sound. The book remains at its original tier.

The station blueprint is now knowledge-gated rather than freely craftable from ingredients alone. Its recipe is unchanged: four Obsidian, two Holy Salt and the Book of Ectcetera as a preserved crafting catalyst. Existing placed stations and pending research continue to function. Old station recipe-book unlocks cannot bypass the new secret gate.

Secrets of the Dead is visible from the starting book. The research station is its first/root entry and remains hidden until revealed. Other entries stay locked under their existing book and personal-Haunt gates; the introduction about awareness still requires the ectoplasm stage. Opening the section does not awaken the book, grant weaving/altar knowledge or change Haunt itself.

Research bonuses can favor this first whisper when it is eligible. After it is learned, a lower-tier book has no further eligible revelation until upgraded, and research waits as before. Existing secret probabilities, benefit cooldowns, field upgrades, sharing and inheritance of genuinely learned books remain otherwise intact.

New knowledge bit 64 expands the same book/player masks from 0–63 to 0–127. Starting/Bone books retain research knowledge only, aware books retain their prior secrets plus research, and purified books allow all seven families. Old saved values remain valid and no old knowledge is discarded. Old legacy full books keep their original weaving knowledge and can receive research as their next new secret; this is not automatically granted to them.

Build and 145 server tests pass. Tests cover a genuine Natural-Haunt event revealing research in a starter book, unchanged book tier, later-gate enforcement, recipe access, persistence/upgrades, legacy knowledge, existing weaving/altar crafting and research-driven selection. Guide/resource validation passes. Whisper feedback and the early book section still need client playtesting.

New players now start at Natural personal Haunt (15.00), so the first whispered secret does not require a developer command to raise their starting tier. Existing saved Haunt is retained.
