# Tapping Haunt audio

The HIT/tapping Haunt effect uses Minecraft's generic wooden impact samples (`minecraft:dig/wood1` through `wood4`), randomly selecting one knock per trigger. No speech or third-party recording is bundled. The stable `mortal_expansion:haunt_tapping` sound event and generated definition allow normal resource-pack overrides.

Playback remains private to the affected player, two blocks behind them, on the Ambient channel at volume 0.8 and pitch 1.0. The subtitle is "Wood knocking". Personal-tier eligibility, protection, prevention notifications, hints and cooldowns are unchanged.

Operator test: `/mortalexpansion haunt effect @s hit`. Remove protective garb and leave wards/blessings or owned loaded Dreamcatcher protection to hear it. Enable Ambient sound volume and subtitles. In-game volume/readability remains a playtest check.

The user-supplied Warframe clip was removed on 2026-10-16. Existing historical commits/notes retain its development history; current builds do not include it. See [audio provenance](THIRD_PARTY_AUDIO.md).
