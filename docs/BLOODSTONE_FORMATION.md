# Bloodstone formation

Every loaded chunk at Dead Rising Haunt or above (30+) receives a Bloodstone conversion roll at dawn (0), sunset (12,000) and midnight (18,000 world-day ticks). A successful roll converts one uniformly selected Diamond Ore or Deepslate Diamond Ore in that chunk. No eligible ore means no conversion. Moon phase does not change these odds. Higher chunk Haunt increases the per-check chance:

| Chunk Haunt | Chance per check | At least one successful roll in three checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Below 30 | 0% | 0% |
| Dead Rising (30-39) | 0.5% (1 in 200) | 1.4925% |
| Demonic (40-49) | 1% (1 in 100) | 2.9701% |
| Abominable (50-59) | 1.5% (about 1 in 66.7) | 4.4328% |
| Eldritch (60-69) | 2% (1 in 50) | 5.8808% |

The three-check totals assume the chunk stays loaded at the same tier and retains eligible ore.

At Dead Rising, three independent checks give a 1.4925% chance of at least one successful roll per fully loaded day, approximately six times the previous 0.25% daily rate. Average first success is 200 eligible checks, roughly 66.7 days, assuming the chunk stays eligible and contains ore.

Existing conversion Haunt charging is preserved: one point is removed only when the planned chunk Haunt exceeds its natural floor. Only already loaded chunks are inspected. Sleeping or moving time forward across scheduled times produces one check, without replaying missed rolls; frozen or backward time produces none. Ordinary Haunt spread retains its separate 8,000-tick schedule, and daily decay, ward cleansing and graveyard weathering retain their daily cadence.
