# Mortal Golem

Apply Rotten Flesh to a placed Bone Block. Each use adds one flesh and consumes one Rotten Flesh, capped at 64 total; creative construction also adds one without consumption. Amounts below 16 are stored but cannot form. The frame and finished golem show four appearances at 16, 32, 48 and 64 (intermediate amounts use the lower completed tier). Exact amounts persist in block entity and entity NBT. Bone orientation is retained. Breaking an unfinished frame returns its Bone Block; applied flesh is consumed construction material.

Finish the desired flesh amount before adding a Wisp o' Lantern above the frame. Placing that head forms the golem immediately. Alternatively, place a Carved Pumpkin there and let its existing nighttime/Supernatural Haunt awakening turn it into a Wisp o' Lantern. That natural conversion still spends its existing one Haunt point. An already-present lantern also forms once sufficient flesh is applied, and loaded lantern ticks retry blocked builds. The two blocks and applied flesh become one golem without item drops. The body occupies approximately 1.4×2.7 blocks: leave clear space around and above it. Obstructions, overlapping entities or canceled entity spawning leave both blocks and all applied flesh intact. No chunk tickets or forced terrain loading are added.

The golem wanders passively for now. It has no combat/retaliation goals, refuses targets and cannot perform melee damage. It is persistent and uses the native golem body/animation APIs with a Wisp pumpkin face and four temporary bone/flesh skins. Flesh currently affects appearance, not statistics; health remains the native golem baseline. There are no new death drops. Its entry is on the Purified book tab, linked from Purification, with no extra secret requirement. World construction itself does not require carrying a book.

New saved records: mortal_expansion:mortal_golem_base block entity with Flesh (0–64) and mortal_expansion:mortal_golem entity with synchronized Flesh (16–64). Existing vanilla blocks/worlds are unchanged until flesh is applied. Native block/entity registration, data serializers and Java model/loot/tag generators are used; there are no new mixins. Client rendering remains in the client package.

## Temporary art

Built-in imagegen produced four bone/flesh material studies from the Iron Golem UV reference. Their torso material samples were exported to 16×16 base faces and packed onto the native 128×128 golem UV faces; the head uses pumpkin rind and the existing Wisp face. This deterministic UV packing keeps all four layouts correct. A front-view texture preview was inspected. Live in-game appearance still needs playtesting.
