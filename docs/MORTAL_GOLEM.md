# Mortal Golem

Apply Rotten Flesh to a placed Bone Block. Each use adds one flesh and consumes one Rotten Flesh, capped at 64 total; creative construction also adds one without consumption. Amounts below 16 are stored but cannot form. The frame and finished golem show four appearances at 16, 32, 48 and 64 (intermediate amounts use the lower completed tier). Exact amounts persist in block entity and entity NBT. Bone orientation is retained. Breaking an unfinished frame returns its Bone Block; applied flesh is consumed construction material.

Finish the desired flesh amount before adding a Wisp o' Lantern above the frame. Placing that head forms the golem immediately. Alternatively, place a Carved Pumpkin there and let its existing nighttime/Supernatural Haunt awakening turn it into a Wisp o' Lantern. That natural conversion still spends its existing one Haunt point. A splash potion containing Regeneration can also awaken a prepared frame with at least 16 flesh and a Carved Pumpkin head in its splash radius, at any time and Haunt tier. It uses the supported projectile-impact event, leaves normal potion effects intact, and consumes the frame and pumpkin only after successful spawn. Blocked or canceled builds remain unchanged; unrelated, drinkable and lingering potions do not awaken it. This route spends no Haunt and does not create a separate Wisp block. An already-present lantern also forms once sufficient flesh is applied, and loaded lantern ticks retry blocked builds. The two blocks and applied flesh become one golem without item drops. The body occupies approximately 1.4×2.7 blocks: leave clear space around and above it. Obstructions, overlapping entities or canceled entity spawning leave both blocks and all applied flesh intact. No chunk tickets or forced terrain loading are added.

The golem hunts entities in Minecraft's `sensitive_to_smite` or the mod's `intangible` entity-type tags, including compatible modded mobs, and otherwise wanders. Players can lure it with Rotten Flesh, Bone, raw Ectoplasm or Purified Ectoplasm in either hand, using the data-generated `mortal_expansion:mortal_golem_lures` item tag and native temptation goal. Combat takes priority over following; luring consumes no items. Players and mobs outside both tags are never attack targets. Spiders (including cave spiders), creepers and endermen cannot target it; this uses NeoForge's target-change event rather than AI mixins. It is persistent and uses the native golem body/animation APIs with a flat Wisp pumpkin face (no nose) and four temporary bone/flesh skins. The four flesh tiers have 25/50/75/100 health. Feeding a damaged golem repairs 1/16 of its maximum health without increasing its flesh count. Feeding a full-health golem adds one flesh (up to 64); new tiers also supply the added maximum-health amount, so a fully healthy golem reaches the same final health whether fleshed before or after awakening. A full-health 64-flesh golem consumes no food. In Dead Rising or higher chunk Haunt, it regenerates 1% maximum health every five seconds without spending Haunt. Repairs never change appearance until the flesh count reaches a new tier. Saved health is retained and clamped to its tier's maximum on load, including old passive golems. There are no new death drops. Its entry is on the Purified book tab, linked from Purification, with no extra secret requirement. World construction itself does not require carrying a book.

New saved records: mortal_expansion:mortal_golem_base block entity with Flesh (0–64) and mortal_expansion:mortal_golem entity with synchronized Flesh (16–64). Existing vanilla blocks/worlds are unchanged until flesh is applied. Native block/entity registration, data serializers and Java model/loot/tag generators are used; the client model layer retains native golem animation and UV coordinates while omitting the nose cube; there are no new mixins. The existing DemiTangibleDamageSourcesMixin also maps Mortal Golem melee to the magic-tagged demi-tangible mob damage type, retaining native golem damage, animations and attacker attribution. Damage events cannot replace the source; this existing factory hook must remain functional on migration. Client rendering remains in the client package.

## Temporary art

Built-in imagegen produced four bone/flesh material studies from the Iron Golem UV reference. Their torso material samples were exported to 16×16 base faces and packed onto the native 128×128 golem UV faces; the head uses pumpkin rind and the existing Wisp face. This deterministic UV packing keeps all four layouts correct. A front-view texture preview was inspected. Live in-game appearance still needs playtesting.
